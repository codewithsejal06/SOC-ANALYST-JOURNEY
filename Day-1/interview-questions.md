## Q1.What is cybersecurity?
Ans : Cybersecurity is the protection of our system including hardware, software, networks, applications, and data from unauthorized access, attacks, damage, or theft.

## Q2.Explain the CIA Triad.
Ans : Confidentiality - in this only authorished person can access the data, can't acces by unauthorished person.
Integrity - data or information should be correct can't alter without permission.
Availabity - data or information should be available when user/authorished person need them.

## Q3.What is a SOC?
Ans : SOC stand fro security oprations center, it is a team of cybersecurity professional who continuosly monitor for suspicious activity and generate respond to security incidents in an organization's system. 

## Q4.What does a SOC Analyst do?
Ans : Monitors alert , detect suspicious activity, investigate incident then respond to attack and fianlly improves security 

## Q5.What is the difference between an Event and an Incident?
Ans : Event is a activity on a system that perfom by user like user log in browser start etc, and Incident is a validate event or alert that confirms a breach of policy, security risk or service disruption.
